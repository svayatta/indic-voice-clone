    Upload a short sample. It is cleaned of noise, then you can hear the voice say anything.

    What makes a good sample

    1. 5 to 45 seconds of speech; 15 to 30 seconds works best.
    2. Only this person speaking: no other voices, music or bells.
    3. Natural speech in their usual dialect, at a steady volume.
    4. Old or noisy recordings are fine: noise and hum are cleaned off before cloning. Heavy echo is the one thing cleaning cannot fix.

    For a clone that keeps the accent closely, add longer recordings after this step and train it.

        Train a stronger clone

        The instant clone copies the voice's colour but often not the accent. Training learns the accent from longer recordings: it needs at least 8 minutes of this person speaking alone, after cleaning and filtering, and takes several hours. Other speakers in the recordings are filtered out using this voice's sample.
